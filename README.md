# cookmitra1 🍳

CookMitra is a premium, AI-powered Indian culinary companion designed to help home cooks turn available ingredients into delicious, budget-friendly meals. It features real-time grocery integration, a multi-language interface, and an interactive "Chef Momo" AI assistant.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js 15 (App Router)
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS & ShadCN UI
- **Animations:** Framer Motion
- **Backend:** Firebase (Firestore & Authentication)
- **AI Logic:** Google Genkit (Gemini 2.5 Flash) for recipe & meal plan generation
- **AI Chat:** Groq SDK (Llama 3.1 8B) for low-latency assistant responses
- **Export:** jsPDF for meal plan and recipe downloads

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. AI-Powered Recipe Generation
Turn your pantry into a feast. Upload a photo or type your ingredients to get a bespoke recipe scaled to your budget and servings.

### 2. Chef Momo AI Assistant
Meet Chef Momo, your voice-supported AI partner. Ask for ingredient substitutions or cooking tips in real-time.

### 3. Healthy Meal Planner
Generate personalized 7-day Indian meal plans based on health goals and weekly budgets, complete with nutritional breakdowns.

### 4. Healing Foods Knowledge Base
Discover ingredients and meals tailored to support specific health conditions (e.g., diabetes-friendly, heart-healthy) based on traditional and modern wisdom.

### 5. Interactive Cook Mode
A distraction-free, step-by-step cooking interface with built-in progress tracking and auto-detected timers.

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Sign Up:** Join to save your favorite AI-crafted recipes.
2. **Pantry:** Add your kitchen staples to "My Pantry" for quick recipe generation.
3. **Generate:** Use the AI Recipe form to create your next meal.
4. **Chat:** Ask Chef Momo anything by clicking the chat icon in the corner!
